About

A geometry found, not taught

I work at the intersection of optimal transport theory and machine learning. My path into it didn't start in a textbook — it started with cell trajectory inference for cultivated meat, modelling proliferating cell populations where mass isn't conserved.

What I derived to fix that turned out to be Wasserstein geometry, arrived at independently before ever reading the formal optimal transport literature. My research thesis: Wasserstein geometry is the correct replacement for Euclidean geometry in probability spaces.
