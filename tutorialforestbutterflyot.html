Tutorial on ForestButterflyOT

September 2026

A first walk through optimal transport: two simple distributions, a cost function, and the intuition behind the transport plan that connects them.

Optimal transport asks a deceptively simple question: what is the cheapest way to move one pile of mass into the shape of another? Before Wasserstein distances, Sinkhorn iterations, or Schrödinger bridges enter the picture, the whole field rests on this one idea — a cost function, a coupling, and a plan. ForestButterflyOT works through that idea from scratch, building the intuition that everything later in Cortex Dreams relies on.
