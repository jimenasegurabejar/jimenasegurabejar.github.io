ForestButterflyOT

The full ButterflyOT universe

Every paper and tutorial from my research on optimal transport lives somewhere in this forest — each one named after the species it grew out of. This page is the index: start here, then follow whichever branch matches what you're trying to understand.

A forest isn't one tree explained well — it's the whole canopy, and how each part connects to the others. Some of these are beginner walkthroughs, some are the papers themselves, and some are half-finished branches still growing. All of them share the same root: treating probability space as the curved thing it actually is, instead of forcing it flat.
