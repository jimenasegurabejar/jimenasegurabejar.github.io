Decentralized Policy Aggregation

Six nodes, scattered across a network, each training on its own slice of the environment — different local data, different compute, updates arriving out of sync. Aggregating their policies by averaging parameters treats the space as flat; aggregating along the Wasserstein barycenter treats it as the curved space it actually is. Turn up node heterogeneity and watch the two methods pull apart.
